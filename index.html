Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about downloading YouTube videos with Ywdown.

Is Ywdown really free?

Yes, 100% free. No subscriptions, no hidden fees, no sign-ups. Convert and download as many public YouTube videos as you want.

What's the highest quality I can download?

Audio: up to 320kbps MP3 or Lossless WAV. Video: up to 4K WEBM. Quality depends on what the original video was uploaded at.

Do I need to install any software?

No. Ywdown runs entirely in your browser. Just paste a link, pick a format, and download. Works on phones, tablets, and desktops.

Can I download private or age-restricted videos?

No. Ywdown only works with public YouTube videos. Private, age-restricted, or region-locked content cannot be downloaded.

Can I trim or cut a video before downloading?

Yes! After converting, switch to the "Trim" tab. Enter start and end times, then download just that part of the video.

Can I download the YouTube thumbnail?

Yes! Switch to the "Thumbnail" tab after converting. You can download the thumbnail in HD, SD, or any available size.

Does Ywdown work on iPhone?

Yes. Ywdown works on iOS, Android, Windows, macOS, and Linux. The layout adapts to any screen size.

Is it legal to download YouTube videos?

The legality depends on the video's license and your local laws. Only download content you own, have permission to use, or that is licensed for reuse. Always respect the creator's terms.
