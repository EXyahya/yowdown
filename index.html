Supported formats and qualities

Ywdown supports every common YouTube output format. Choose based on what you plan to do with the file:

  • MP3 (64–320 kbps) — best for music, podcasts, audiobooks, and any audio-only use
  • M4A (128 kbps) — alternative audio format, natively supported by Apple devices
  • WAV (Lossless) — uncompressed audio for studio and editing workflows
  • MP4 (360p–4K) — universal video format for phones, tablets, laptops, and TVs
  • WEBM (1080p, 4K) — open video format with excellent compression at high resolutions

The actual quality depends on the original video's source stream. If a video was uploaded at 720p, you can't get 4K from it. Always pick the highest setting that matches the source for the best balance of quality and file size.

Best audio settings for different uses

For spoken content like podcasts, interviews, and audiobooks, 128 kbps MP3 is plenty. It offers clear speech with a small file size. For music, 192 kbps MP3 is the sweet spot — good quality, reasonable file size. For high-fidelity listening with good headphones, choose 256 or 320 kbps MP3. If you need studio-grade audio, WAV (Lossless) preserves every detail, but the files are much larger.

Best video settings for different devices

For phones with limited storage or slow internet, 360p or 480p MP4 is great — small files, quick downloads. For laptops and monitors, 720p or 1080p MP4 looks sharp on most screens. For 4K TVs or large displays, choose 1440p MP4 or 4K WEBM when the source supports it. Higher resolution means bigger files, so pick what matches your screen.
