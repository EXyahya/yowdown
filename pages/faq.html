Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about downloading high quality MP3 and MP4 from YouTube with Ywdown.

General

What is Ywdown?

Ywdown is a free, browser-based YouTube converter. It lets you download public YouTube videos as MP3 audio files or MP4 video files, without installing any software or creating an account. Just paste a URL, pick a format and quality, and save the file to your device.

Is Ywdown really free?

Yes. Ywdown is completely free to use. There are no subscriptions, no hidden fees, and no sign-ups. You can convert as many public videos as you want, as often as you want.

Do I need to install an app or extension?

No. Ywdown runs entirely in your web browser. Just open the page, paste a YouTube URL, choose a format and quality, and click Download. No apps, no browser extensions, no plugins required.

Formats and Quality

What audio formats does Ywdown support?

Ywdown supports MP3 (64–320 kbps), M4A (128 kbps), and WAV (Lossless). For most uses, MP3 at 192 kbps gives the best balance of file size and sound quality. For audiophiles with good headphones, 320 kbps MP3 or WAV Lossless preserves more detail.

What video formats does Ywdown support?

Ywdown supports MP4 from 360p up to 4K, and WEBM up to 4K when the source video supports it. Choose MP4 for universal compatibility with phones, tablets, and TVs. Choose WEBM for smaller files at the same resolution.

Can I download 4K videos?

Yes, when the source video has a 4K stream available. Ywdown will offer the highest quality that the original video supports. You cannot get 4K from a video uploaded at 1080p — the output quality is always limited by the source.

What's the best audio quality for music?

For most music listening, 192 kbps MP3 is the sweet spot — good quality with a reasonable file size. For high-fidelity listening on quality headphones or speakers, 256 or 320 kbps MP3 preserves more detail. WAV (Lossless) is for studio use and editing.

Usage

How do I download a YouTube video?

  1. Open YouTube in your browser and copy the video URL.
  2. Paste the URL into the Ywdown search box.
  3. Pick a format (MP3 for audio, MP4 for video) and a quality.
  4. Click Download and save the file to your device.

For a more detailed walkthrough, see our How to Download guide.

Can I use Ywdown on my phone?

Yes. Ywdown works on Android phones and tablets, iPhones and iPads. The layout is mobile-friendly, and downloads go to your device's Downloads folder. On iOS, downloaded files appear in the Files app under Downloads.

Can I download private or age-restricted videos?

No. Ywdown only works with public YouTube videos. Private, age-restricted, members-only, or region-locked videos cannot be processed.

Where are my files saved?

Files are saved to your browser's default download folder. On desktop, check your Downloads folder. On Android, check the Downloads app. On iOS, check the Files app under Downloads.

Safety and Privacy

Does Ywdown store my downloads?

No. Ywdown does not store any downloaded files on its servers. Files are streamed directly to your device. We don't log what you download, and we don't tie downloads to your identity.

Does Ywdown collect personal data?

Ywdown does not require an account, email, or any personal information. Anonymous usage analytics may be collected to improve the service, but no personal identifiers are stored.

Are the downloaded files safe?

Yes. Downloaded files are exactly what YouTube serves — MP3, MP4, WEBM, or similar media files. Always check the file extension after download. Don't open files ending in .exe, .msi, or .bat — those are not from Ywdown.

Legal

Is it legal to download YouTube videos?

The legality of downloading YouTube videos depends on the video's license and your local laws. You should only download content that you own, that you have permission to use, or that is licensed for reuse (such as Creative Commons). Downloading does not transfer copyright to you, and you should not redistribute downloaded content without the creator's permission.

Can I use downloaded videos commercially?

Generally no, unless you have explicit permission from the copyright holder or the content is licensed for commercial use. Always review the license and contact the creator if you're unsure.
